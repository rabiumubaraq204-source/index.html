Introduction to the Cell

A short sample note to demonstrate how learning materials could be organised.

What is a cell?

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. Some organisms consist of one cell, while others are made up of many cells.

Key cell structures

  • Cell membrane: controls the movement of substances into and out of the cell.
  • Nucleus: contains most of the cell's genetic material in eukaryotic cells.
  • Mitochondria: are important sites of ATP production during aerobic respiration.
  • Ribosomes: are responsible for protein synthesis.

Quick revision

Try to explain the function of each structure in your own words before attempting the CBT section.
